# Tools

This section holds the checklist that turns The Ask-Then-Check Loop into something you can do at a desk, and the one page that holds any figure set by law, regulation, or an agency. Each tool supports a specific step and explains what to do with the result.

## What belongs here

A checklist makes sure nothing is skipped when a step has many parts. A reference page holds the numbers that change, so the rest of the site can name a concept and point here. This guide needs few such numbers, so the reference page is short by design.

## What's here

**[This year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures)** lists every limit, rate, threshold, and deadline the Foundation pages refer to, with the year each applies to and its primary source, and is refreshed once a year.

**[Checking an answer](/tools/checking-an-answer)** is a checklist for the fifth step of the loop: what to test in an answer about money, in what order, and what to do when a test fails.
