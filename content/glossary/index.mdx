# Glossary

This page defines every term the guide links to, in plain English and for education rather than advice. Any number a term depends on, such as a limit, rate, or age, lives on [This year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures) rather than here.

### Primary source [#primary-source]

The original place a fact comes from rather than a summary of it: the statute, the agency's own page or form, the lender's own agreement, or the research paper. For a money rule, it is the document a court or regulator would point to, not an article or an answer about it.
